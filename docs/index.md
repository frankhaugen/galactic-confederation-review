<div markdown="1">

The **Galactic Confederation Review** republishes selected works that travel usefully across polity boundaries. Selections enter the archive as they clear review; series and dossiers help readers follow subjects without pretending the Review publishes fixed issues.

The Review is funded under Confederation mandate, but editorially independent. Selection for republication does not imply endorsement by the Review, the Galactic Confederation, or the originating polity.
